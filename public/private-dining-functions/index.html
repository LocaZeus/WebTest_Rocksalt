How many people can you host?

Rocksalt seats 50 to 60 indoors, or 70 for cocktail and canapé events.

Is there a minimum spend?

No. There's no minimum spend for private dining and functions.

Does sending an enquiry confirm my booking?

No. An enquiry starts the conversation. We'll talk through the options with you, and you confirm the arrangements directly with the venue.

What if I don't have a date yet?

That's fine. Tick "Not sure yet" on the form and tell us roughly when you're thinking.

Can you cater for allergies?

Please tell our team in advance. Our kitchen handles nuts, gluten and other allergens, and cross-contact may occur.

Is it just for a table of friends?

If you only need a table, use Book a table for a regular reservation. Functions have their own enquiry form above.
