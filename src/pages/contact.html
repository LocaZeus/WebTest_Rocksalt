Do I need to book?

You can walk in or book a table online through OpenTable. Sunday live music is walk-in with no cover, so book a table separately if you'd like dinner.

What are your opening hours?

Every day from 7:30am to 8:30pm. On public holidays we open from 8am to 3pm, and a 15% surcharge applies.

Until when is breakfast served?

The breakfast menu runs until 3pm every day.

Can you cater for allergies?

Please tell our team before ordering. Our kitchen handles nuts, gluten and other allergens, and cross-contact may occur.

Do you have vegetarian and gluten free dishes?

Yes. The menu marks vegetarian (V), gluten free (GF) and vegetarian option available (VOA) dishes.

Can we hold a private function?

Yes. Rocksalt seats 50 to 60 indoors, or 70 for cocktail and canapé events, with no minimum spend. Send a function enquiry or call to talk it through.

How do we get there?

We're at 360-364 Bay Street, Brighton, a short walk from North Brighton station. Call us with any parking or access questions.
